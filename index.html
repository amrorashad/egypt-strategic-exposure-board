How to read status labels

Confirmed: supported by an original authoritative record. Corroborated: multiple credible reports. Disputed: contested statements attributed to involved parties. Unknown: insufficient evidence. “No verified incident” means evidence reviewed has not established one; it does not prove one did not occur.
