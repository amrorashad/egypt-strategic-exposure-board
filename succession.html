Evidence cutoff: 30 October 2026 · Baseline: CIQ-GEO-2026-10-14

Executive assessment

President Abdel Fattah El-Sisi's 17 October 2026 public commitment to leave office in 2030 and proposal for a 30-month Military Academy leadership programme are the reference events. No enacted candidate-selection mechanism, final committee appointments or presidential successor designation is established in this record.

Do not confuse the Academy proposal with constitutional presidential eligibility or an enacted law.

Priority individual: Hassan Mahmoud Rashad

Analyst-mentioned possibility · NOT a declared candidate

  • Confirmed office: Director of Egypt's General Intelligence Service, appointed 16 October 2024, succeeding Abbas Kamel.
  • Attributed analysis: Charles W. Dunne was reported by Alhurra on 23 October 2026 as identifying Rashad among possible successors. This is an analyst's assessment, not an official nomination or endorsement.
  • Monitoring signals: official role changes, credible public candidacy statements, formal selection committee appointments, published legal criteria, and corroborated institutional decisions.

Alhurra original Arabic article (23 October 2026) · English edition

Institutional watchlist

Presidency; Ministry of Defence; Armed Forces chief of staff; General Intelligence Service; Egyptian Military Academy; House of Representatives; National Elections Authority. Officeholders' influence over succession must not be assumed from their titles alone.

What would constitute a material change?

  • Published eligibility, selection, or committee rules with legal authority
  • Formal appointments or changes in electoral law
  • Verified declaration or restriction of a credible candidate
  • Documented reversal of the 2030 departure commitment

Strategic implications — scenarios, not findings

Institutional continuity: transparent, enforceable rules could reduce transition uncertainty. Political competition: exclusive or opaque screening could constrain it. Investor confidence: credible continuity may reassure; elite conflict could heighten uncertainty. Stability: no internal institutional confrontation is established by this evidence.
